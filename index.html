Main project · Real-time Voice AI · Streaming

AI Voice Agent for Lead Qualification

A real-time voice agent that handles inbound and outbound phone calls and qualifies leads in natural conversation. Live call audio is streamed end to end. Plivo carries the telephony, Sarvam AI transcribes speech in Indian regional languages, an LLM agent drives the qualification dialogue, and Cartesia streams the spoken reply back to the caller.

  • Inbound + outbound call flows on Plivo with bidirectional audio streaming over WebSockets.
  • Voice Activity Detection for natural turn-taking: knowing when the caller has finished, and handling interruptions.
  • Latency engineering: every stage streams (STT → LLM → TTS) so the agent answers without awkward silences.

Impact Automated lead qualification over the phone, in the languages Indian customers actually speak.
